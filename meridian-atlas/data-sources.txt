Meridian Atlas — reference geography
Snapshot assembled on 24 November 2026. Installed-edition records are in geo-book-1.0.8.0.json. Publicly documented examples are in game-guide.json.

Names, places and units
World records include countries, dependencies and other mapped regions. Names and administrative updates
Naoero is the atlas and study headword. Nauru remains a search alias and the spelling in the original game files. Equatorial Guinea moved its capital to Ciudad de la Paz in January 2026. Sri Lanka's administrative capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte. The Svalbard and Jan Mayen area figure is the sum of Statistics Norway's 2026 land and fresh water areas.

Pronunciation
Phonemic IPA uses one English reading per place, in broad American English. Contributors to English Wiktionary and Wikipedia supplied geographical entries. For individual contributors and revisions, open the article for the spelling in question and choose View history. IPA has been selected and adapted for this atlas, including American English word readings from ipa-dict and CMUdict. Some less widely documented names use an English approximation of the local pronunciation. The Wikimedia-derived transcriptions are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Chinese introductions annotate recognized names on their first occurrence in a paragraph, omitting annotations for the entry itself and its geographic context. The spellings and readings reuse the atlas entries where available, with a separate glossary for names that occur only in the text. English Harbour retains the spelling used in the UNESCO listing.
English names for selected wildlife and plants follow references from South African National Parks, SANBI and the UNESCO World Heritage Centre.

Rivers
River lines and their connections across lakes use Natural Earth's 1:50m Rivers and Lake Centerlines v5.0.0. The bundled layer omits canals, simplifies each line to a tolerance of 0.012 degrees, and draws scale ranks 1–3 with a heavier stroke.
English water labels use the same river and lake layers and Natural Earth's 1:50m marine areas v5.1.0. Lake and marine labels use interior points. Major rivers have alternative anchors along their course so their names can move past crowded labels when zooming. Viewport size and zoom determine which labels fit.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-physical-vectors/50m-rivers-lake-centerlines/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-physical-vectors/50m-physical-labels/
