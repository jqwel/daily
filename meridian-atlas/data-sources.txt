Meridian Atlas — reference geography
Snapshot assembled on 24 November 2026. Installed-edition records are in geo-book-1.0.8.0.json. Publicly documented examples are in game-guide.json.

Names, places and units
World records include countries, dependencies and other mapped regions. Names, aliases, representative coordinates, areas and administrative centres are adapted from world-countries 5.1.0 by Mohammed Le Doze and contributors. The adapted geographical database is available under ODbL 1.0. Place coordinates are representative points for the region. Areas are given in square kilometres.
https://github.com/mledoze/countries
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Names and administrative updates
The UN name Naoero is indexed under its previous name Nauru. Equatorial Guinea moved its capital to Ciudad de la Paz in January 2026. Sri Lanka's administrative capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte. U.S. state facts
Areas and internal coordinates come from the 2010 Census state area table. Populations are July 1 estimates from the Census Bureau's Vintage 2024 series as reproduced by Rhode Island's Department of Labor and Training. State capitals are listed by USDA NASS. The District of Columbia is a federal district.

Pronunciation
Phonemic IPA uses one English reading per place, in broad American English. Contributors to English Wiktionary and Wikipedia supplied geographical entries. For individual contributors and revisions, open the article for the spelling in question and choose View history. IPA has been selected and adapted for this atlas, including American English word readings from ipa-dict and CMUdict. Some less widely documented names use an English approximation of the local pronunciation. The Wikimedia-derived transcriptions are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:Search

Globe and phonetics
The flat and orthographic views share the atlas region identifiers. Globe geometry is adapted from the same geographic sources. D3 and TopoJSON project and clip it locally; runtime license notices are in assets/licenses. The broad transcription uses /r/ for the English approximant conventionally written [ɹ], with /ɚ/ and /ɝ/ for r-coloured vowels.
https://d3js.org/d3-geo
https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/content/ipa-chart
